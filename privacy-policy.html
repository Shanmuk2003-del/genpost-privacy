# Privacy Policy — Genpost **Last updated: October 10, 2026** Genpost ("we", "our", or "the application") is an application designed to help users generate images and captions using AI and publish content to LinkedIn when explicitly requested by the user. This Privacy Policy explains how Genpost processes information when you use the application. ## 1. Information We Process Depending on the features you use, Genpost may process: - Text prompts submitted for AI content generation. - Images uploaded by the user. - AI-generated images and captions. - LinkedIn account information returned through LinkedIn's authorization and API services, such as a member identifier and available profile information. - OAuth access tokens required to perform actions authorized by the user. - Application settings, draft content, and diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the application. Please do not submit sensitive personal information in prompts unless it is necessary and appropriate for your intended use. ## 2. How We Use Information Genpost processes information to: - Generate images and captions requested by the user. - Process uploaded images and prepare content for LinkedIn. - Authenticate the user's LinkedIn account. - Upload images and publish posts when explicitly requested by the user. - Store drafts and application data needed for the requested functionality. - Maintain application security, diagnose errors, and improve reliability. Genpost is designed not to publish content without an explicit user action requesting publication. ## 3. LinkedIn Data and Authorization Genpost uses LinkedIn's authentication and API services to provide LinkedIn publishing functionality. When you authorize Genpost, LinkedIn may provide information and permissions according to the scopes displayed during authorization. Genpost uses the resulting authorization to perform only the actions required for the application's enabled functionality. LinkedIn handles its own processing of account information under LinkedIn's applicable terms and privacy policy. You can revoke Genpost's authorization through LinkedIn's account or application settings, where available. Revoking authorization may prevent further publishing until you authorize the application again. ## 4. AI and Third-Party Services Genpost may send prompts, images, or related content to third-party AI providers to perform requested generation or processing tasks. LinkedIn receives information required for authorized image uploads and publishing. The specific information processed by a provider depends on the feature used and the provider's integration. Those providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies. Users should review the relevant provider policies before submitting information they consider confidential or sensitive. ## 5. Storage and Security Genpost is designed for local use. Application data, drafts, images, and authorization tokens may be stored on the user's device, depending on the enabled features and configuration. OAuth tokens are intended to be stored locally using operating-system-supported protections where available. Users should protect their computer, application directory, and backups against unauthorized access. Temporary files may remain until removed by the application or the user. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain application records, troubleshoot problems, or meet applicable legal requirements. Users can delete locally stored drafts, images, and application data when those files are no longer needed. Removing an authorization token locally does not necessarily revoke the corresponding authorization at LinkedIn; users should also revoke access through LinkedIn. Deleting local content does not automatically delete posts already published to LinkedIn. Published content must be managed through LinkedIn or its supported APIs. ## 7. Sharing and Sale of Information Genpost does not sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to LinkedIn and AI or infrastructure providers when necessary to deliver a feature requested by the user. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. ## 8. Children's Privacy Genpost is not intended for children who do not meet the minimum age requirements applicable to the services used by the application. Genpost does not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. ## 9. Your Choices and Rights Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request information about personal data associated with your use of Genpost or request its deletion. You can stop using Genpost, delete locally stored application data, and revoke LinkedIn authorization. Some information may remain with third-party providers or on LinkedIn according to their respective policies and retention practices. ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as Genpost's functionality or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when revisions are published. ## 11.
